# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview
This documentation set describes the project management processes OctoAcme uses to plan, execute, track, and improve work across cross-functional teams.

## Project management process summary
OctoAcme follows a lightweight lifecycle for project work: initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. During initiation, the team confirms the business problem, stakeholders, goals, and success metrics. Planning breaks work into deliverables, milestones, and backlog items with clear ownership and acceptance criteria. Execution emphasizes tracking progress, managing dependencies, holding routine check-ins, and escalating blockers when needed. Release and retrospective activities ensure quality, learning, and continuous improvement after delivery.

## How these docs fit together
The OctoAcme project management approach centers on clear roles, visible progress, and lightweight governance. Teams start by validating the need and aligning stakeholders, then translate the initiative into a roadmap and backlog. During execution, the team tracks milestones, risks, and blockers while maintaining communication and quality gates. After delivery, the team reviews outcomes and captures lessons to improve the next cycle.
